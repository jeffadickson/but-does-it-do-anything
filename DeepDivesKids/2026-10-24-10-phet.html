Meet the idea

PhET offers interactive science and math simulations that turn abstract ideas into experiments.

Imagine this: play with forces and motion. What would you make first?

How it works

Sliders and draggable objects change a model's variables; the simulation responds with visual feedback.

Try predicting: What changes when you give the program a different instruction or number?

Try it!

Here is a tiny physics calculation to change and discuss: force divided by mass gives acceleration.

const forceNewtons = 12;
const massKg = 3;
const acceleration = forceNewtons / massKg;
console.log(acceleration + ' m/s²');

Challenge: Change one number or direction, predict the result, and record what happened. If this is pseudocode or a partial library example, use the linked official environment or tutorial.

What makes it tricky?

A simulation is a teaching model and can omit friction, air resistance, or real-world complexity.
